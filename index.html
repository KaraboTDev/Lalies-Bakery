About the bakery

Lalies Bakery is based in Thohoyandou and bakes to order , every wedding cake, birthday cake, batch of scones and batch of biscuits is made for a specific date rather than kept sitting on a shelf.

Scones and biscuits are also available in bulk quantities, measured by the litre, which makes them a practical choice for church events, school functions and large gatherings.

Wedding and birthday cakes are made to order , get in touch with your date, size and any design preferences, and we'll confirm what's possible and the price.
