Orders and quotes

Prices listed on this site are starting prices. The final price for a wedding cake, birthday cake, or bulk order of scones or biscuits depends on size, design, quantity and any special requests, and will be confirmed with you before your order is accepted.

Order deadlines

Wedding cakes and large bulk orders require advance notice. Please contact us as early as possible with your date so we can confirm availability.

Allergies

If you have any food allergies or dietary requirements, please tell us when you order. While we take care in preparation, our kitchen handles common allergens and we cannot guarantee any product is completely free of them.
