Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026/09/11

This policy explains what information Lalies Bakery collects from you when you use this website or place an order, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

When you submit the order form or contact us directly, we collect the details you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, event date and order details.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your order request and confirm pricing and availability
  • To communicate with you about an order you have placed
  • To keep basic records of orders for our own bakery planning

We do not sell your information to third parties.

How we store your information

Information submitted through this website is used only for the purpose of fulfilling your order and is not shared beyond what is needed to prepare and deliver it.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, request that it be corrected, and request that it be deleted, subject to any records we are required to keep for legitimate business reasons.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us using the details on the order page.
